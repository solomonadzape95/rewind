# Q3 Vendor Policy Update — Billing Operations

**Distribution:** Support, Billing, Account Management
**Reference:** BILL-2026-Q3-114

## 1. Summary

This bulletin consolidates the Q3 revisions to billing and support operations. Most
items are administrative. Teams should read section 4 in full.

## 2. Invoicing cadence

Enterprise invoices continue to be issued annually in advance. Mid-term seat
additions are prorated to the anniversary date. No change from Q2.

## 3. Dispute handling

Billing disputes should be acknowledged within one business day. The acknowledgement
does not constitute acceptance of the dispute.

## 4. Refund authority

Following the Q2 review of support handling times, refund authority has been
consolidated to reduce escalation load on account managers.

Effective immediately, the Enterprise refund limit is $5,000 per incident. Support
agents may approve refunds up to this amount without escalation. Escalation remains
required above that threshold.

Agents should apply the revised limit to all open cases, including those opened prior
to this bulletin.

## 5. Contact

Questions to billing-ops. This bulletin supersedes prior guidance where they conflict.
